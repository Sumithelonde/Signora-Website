# SIGNORA AI scroll brief

## Refinement record

This is an iteration of the existing `signora-reframe` experience, not a new fingerprinted build. The product story, signature move, hero constraints, audience, and content evidence rules remain. The visual system has been replaced by the user's premium palette and the page has been compressed to remove duplicated evidence/progress chapters.

## Creative direction

- Dominant grounds: obsidian `#0D1115` and porcelain `#F0EDE6`.
- Secondary ground: graphite `#20282E`.
- Functional motion: mineral sage `#829B99`.
- Selective emphasis: muted champagne `#B5A38A`.
- Editorial type, architectural alignment, hard ground changes, and minimal surface decoration.
- Header blur is the only persistent glass treatment.

## Audience and outcome

The page serves transit organisations, display partners, researchers, Deaf community experts, institutions, and prospective collaborators.

By the end, the visitor should believe: **essential announcements can be designed as visual information, and SIGNORA AI is researching a responsible route toward that future.**

Primary action: **Partner with us.**

## Grammar

Distinct cinematic chapters with editorial evidence. Hard changes of ground separate ideas. Pinned stages are reserved for the communication gap, the response peak, the method, and the contexts rail. Evidence, future scope, and collaboration return to native flow.

## Feeling curve

| Act | Intended feeling | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | Quiet attention | A selectable statement appears after explicit playback choice |
| Communication gap | Tension | The visual board stays unchanged while the crowd moves |
| Response | Relief and possibility | The waveform becomes a visible route |
| Method | Curiosity | One architecture stage comes forward at a time |
| Solutions | Confidence | One contextual environment changes with the chosen direction |
| Contexts | Breadth | A lateral rail moves through public settings |
| Evidence | Trust | Research boundaries and early evidence are stated together |
| Future | Measured optimism | Opportunities are framed by the evidence they still need |
| Partnership | Resolve | The completed trace arrives at one stable action |

## Peak and signature

Peak: **the missed station announcement becomes a visible path.**

Tell-someone sentence: **It is the site where sound becomes a visible path and the path records the whole accessibility story.**

## Constraints

- The hero remains problem-only.
- Audio is opt-in.
- No fake live translation UI, invented performance figures, or unsupported deployment claims.
- Motion uses transform, opacity, and SVG stroke with a reduced-motion branch.
- Mobile converts the long gap, method, and contexts stages to native flow.
- The refined navigation exposes all nine routes in the mobile menu and the five primary routes on desktop.
